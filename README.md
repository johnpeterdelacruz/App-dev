# My favorite movie: How to Make Millions Before Grandma Dies
## about the movie 
its a **Thai drama film** about a young man named M who takes care of his grandmother in hopes of receiving her inheritance.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because:
- It shows the importance of family.
- It teaches us to appreciate our grandparents.
- The story is emotional and meaningful.
- It reminds us that **time with our loved ones is more valuable than money**.

## My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **M** because he learns the importance of love, care, and family throughout the story.

## My Favorite Lesson
> Family is more important than money, and we should appreciate our loved ones while they are still with us.
